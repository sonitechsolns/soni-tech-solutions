LIVE EVENT PLATFORM SNAPNXS // 01

SNAPNEXUS

A live photo wall for weddings, parties and college events. Guests scan a QR code, snap a photo, and it appears on the venue's TV or projector within seconds. No app to install, no account to create, and the host gets every photo at the end of the night.

WHAT I BUILT
  • Guest upload page, host dashboard and live TV wall with five layouts
  • Photo moderation, private groups and one-click ZIP download of every photo
  • UPI checkout with a free 1-hour trial, ₹49 per event and a ₹999/month Pro plan

DATACENTER 3D TWIN SPARSHIKA // 02

SPARSHIKA

A live 3D copy of a datacenter, built from the hardware itself. When a fan slows down or a power supply fails, Sparshika shows exactly which part, in which rack, and flies you straight to it. "Sparsha" means touch.

WHAT I BUILT
  • A walkable 3D hall of server racks, with failing parts, cabling and a heat map
  • An on-site agent that reads real server sensors (IPMI, SNMP) and reports to a cloud API
  • Sign-up with STS approval, one-time agent enrolment codes and team roles
